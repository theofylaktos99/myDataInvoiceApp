## 🚧 Risk Analysis

### ⚠️ High Risk Factors

1. **myDATA API Changes**
   - **Risk:** Government changes requirements
   - **Impact:** High - Core functionality affected
   - **Mitigation:** Close monitoring, quick adaptation, government liaison

2. **Competitive Response**
   - **Risk:** Large players price dumping
   - **Impact:** Medium - Market pressure
   - **Mitigation:** Feature differentiation, customer loyalty, niche focus

3. **Economic Downturn**
   - **Risk:** SMEs reduce software spending
   - **Impact:** High - Reduced sales
   - **Mitigation:** Flexible pricing, value demonstration, essential positioning

### ⚠️ Medium Risk Factors

4. **Technical Debt**
   - **Risk:** MVP code needs major refactoring
   - **Impact:** Medium - Development slowdown
   - **Mitigation:** Gradual refactoring, test coverage, documentation

5. **Customer Acquisition Cost**
   - **Risk:** Higher than expected CAC
   - **Impact:** Medium - Reduced profitability  
   - **Mitigation:** Multiple channels, referral programs, content marketing

6. **Regulatory Compliance**
   - **Risk:** Data protection, tax law changes
   - **Impact:** Medium - Compliance costs
   - **Mitigation:** Legal consultation, compliance-first design

### ⚠️ Low Risk Factors

7. **Team Scaling**
   - **Risk:** Difficulty hiring quality developers
   - **Impact:** Low - Growth slowdown
   - **Mitigation:** Remote work, competitive packages, gradual scaling

8. **Technology Obsolescence**
   - **Risk:** React/tech stack becomes outdated
   - **Impact:** Low - Maintenance overhead
   - **Mitigation:** Regular updates, modern architecture, migration planning
